I am a PhD student at the Language Technologies Institute at **[Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/)**, advised by [**Prof. Graham Neubig**](https://phontron.com).

My research explores building reliable and capable AI agents. I primarily focus on: 1) evaluating agents for reliable autonomous scientific discovery \[[Reverse Engineering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17968), [EurekaBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00492)\], 2) agents that can reliably adapt and evolve across long-horizon interactions \[[LM Belief Shifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01805)\], and 3) multi-agent systems where agents with diverse skills and experiences collaborate effectively \[[CAID](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21489)\].

Griffiths**](https://cocosci.princeton.edu/tom/index.php), and my Bachelor's degree at **[McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/)**, advised by [**Prof. Xue (Steve) Liu**](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~xueliu/site/intro.html) and [**Prof. Eric D. Kolaczyk**](https://www.mcgill.ca/mathstat/eric-d-kolaczyk).
